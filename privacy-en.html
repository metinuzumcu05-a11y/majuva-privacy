About Majuva

Majuva is an Android app for creating and managing shopping lists on your device. Core shopping features work offline. There are no user accounts, sign-in, cloud synchronization or shared lists. This policy covers earlier ad-free versions and newer versions incorporating advertising and Firebase services. The online services described below apply only to versions that include them.

Data stored on your device

List IDs and names, selected icons and colors, last-used timestamps, product IDs, quantities, units, ordering, purchased status and shopping history are stored in Android app-private local storage. List names are user-entered and may contain personal information. Search text, category and tab selection are not part of persistent list records. The catalog, icons and font are bundled with the app. Privacy preferences are also stored on the device.

Use and sharing of data

Shopping records are used locally to display, edit and preserve your lists. The app does not send list names, product records or search text to GrapeByte Studios servers or to advertising and Firebase services. Google services may process technical data as described below.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Ad-supported versions use the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request non-personalized banner ads. Google and relevant advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, accessible device or app identifiers, app and ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information. Purposes include ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads do not mean that no data is transmitted. Shopping content is not used for ad targeting. The app is not designed to request precise location permission.

Advertising consent and privacy choices

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may communicate with Google on app launch to update privacy status and show consent messages where required; this may occur before an ad request. Consent preferences may be stored on the device. Where required, the Ad privacy choices option in Settings allows you to change or withdraw consent. Ad requests are subject to UMP eligibility checks. Declining consent does not necessarily mean no ads will be shown; limited or non-personalized ads may be served where eligible. Android advertising preferences can also be managed in device settings.

Firebase Analytics — usage statistics

Usage statistics are off by default. If you enable Usage statistics in Settings, Google Analytics may process standard usage events and technical information such as app instance identifiers, device and operating system information, approximate location and usage interactions. List creation, product addition and shopping completion events are recorded without content parameters. List names, product names or IDs, quantities, search text and shopping records are not included in these events. Analytics advertising ID collection and automatic screen reporting are disabled in the app configuration. Turning this setting off is intended to stop new analytics collection; it does not automatically erase previously transmitted data.

Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting

Crash reporting is off by default and is independent of usage statistics. Crashlytics may create local crash records; automatic uploading is disabled. Pending reports are queued for submission on a subsequent launch if valid permission exists, or deletion is requested if permission is absent. Reports may contain technical crash details, device and operating system characteristics, app version, installation identifiers and stack traces. App-generated Flutter reports do not include raw error messages or shopping records. Reports already submitted to the SDK or transmitted cannot be recalled by turning off the preference.

Firebase Core and Remote Config

Firebase infrastructure and Remote Config are used to retrieve remote configuration settings controlling whether ads are enabled. Firebase installation IDs, app and version information and technical network data may be processed. This configuration communication is independent of the optional usage statistics and crash reporting choices. Shopping lists are not uploaded to the cloud. Core shopping features continue to work without a network connection.

Android system backups

There is no in-app cloud synchronization. Android system backup has not been explicitly disabled. Depending on the device, Android version, manufacturer and settings, local list records and recovery copies may be backed up or transferred by the operating system. This is not a transfer to GrapeByte Studios. System backups are managed through Android or the relevant backup provider. GrapeByte Studios cannot remotely delete them or control their retention periods.

Recovery copies of damaged records

When an unreadable or partially damaged record is detected, a local copy of the original raw record may be kept to prevent data loss. It may contain list names, products, quantities and purchased status. If reading fails, retry is offered instead of overwriting existing records with a default list. Recovery copies have no automatic expiry and a newer copy may replace an older one. Deleting a list or item does not automatically erase an earlier recovery copy.

Retention and deletion

List records may remain until deleted by the user, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Lists and products can be deleted in the app. Android Settings > Apps > Majuva > Storage > Clear data removes main and recovery records on the device (menu names may vary). Uninstalling removes app-private local data; system backups are managed separately and may restore it. There is no account-deletion flow because there are no user accounts. GrapeByte Studios cannot remotely erase lists on your device. Retention and deletion of data sent to Google services are subject to the relevant service settings, policies and legal obligations. You may contact us below with related requests.

Security and international data processing

Records are stored in Android app-private storage; the app does not add a separate encryption layer. Device locking and Android security protections are important. Online Google services may process data outside your country. Absolute security or recovery in every circumstance cannot be guaranteed.

Contact and data rights

For privacy and support, contact grapebytestudios@gmail.com. Subject to applicable law, you may submit requests concerning your personal data, including information, access, correction, deletion, objection or withdrawal of consent. Information voluntarily shared by email may be processed and retained by the email provider to respond to your request. You may request deletion of correspondence at the same address, subject to applicable legal retention obligations. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised.

Changes

When data processing practices, advertising services or privacy-related features change, this policy and relevant Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated. The current text is published on this page.
