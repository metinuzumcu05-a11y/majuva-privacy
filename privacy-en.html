About Majuva

Majuva is an offline Android app for creating and managing shopping lists on your device. This version has no user accounts, sign-in, cloud synchronization, or shared lists.

Data stored on your device

Majuva stores list IDs and names, selected list icons and colors, last-used timestamps, product IDs, quantities, units, ordering, and purchased status in Android's app-private local storage. List names are user-entered and may contain personal information. Search text, category and tab selection are not part of the persistent list record in this version. The product catalog, icons, and font are bundled with the app.

Use and sharing of data

Data is used locally to display, edit, and preserve your lists. The reviewed Majuva 1.0 app does not send list or usage data to GrapeByte Studios' servers or share it with advertising or analytics services. It has no Firebase, advertising, or analytics SDK. Product images are not downloaded from the internet.

Android system backups

There is no in-app cloud sync, but Android system backup has not been explicitly disabled. Depending on your device, Android version, manufacturer, and settings, local list records and recovery copies may be backed up or transferred by the operating system. This is not a transfer to GrapeByte Studios. You manage system backups through Android or the backup provider. GrapeByte Studios cannot remotely delete these backups or control their retention period.

Recovery copies of damaged records

When an unreadable or partially damaged record is detected, Majuva may keep a local copy of the original raw record to prevent accidental loss. It may contain names, items, quantities, and purchased status. If reading fails, the app offers retry rather than overwriting existing records with a default list. Recovery copies have no automatic expiry; a newer copy may replace an older one. Deleting an item or list does not automatically erase data from an earlier recovery copy.

Retention and deletion

Ordinary list data may remain until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall Majuva. Lists and items can be deleted in the app. Android Settings > Apps > Majuva > Storage > Clear data removes the main and recovery records on the device (menu names may vary). Uninstalling removes app-private local data, but Android system backups are managed separately and may later restore it. The app has no account-deletion flow because it has no user accounts. GrapeByte Studios cannot remotely erase lists on your device.

Security

Data is held in Android's app-private storage. The app does not add a separate encryption layer; device locking and Android security protections matter. Absolute security and recovery in every circumstance cannot be guaranteed.

Contact and support email

Contact metinuzumcu05@gmail.com with privacy or support questions. Information you voluntarily email may be processed and retained by the email provider to respond to your request; this is separate from automatic data collection by the app. You may request deletion of correspondence at the same address, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised.

Changes

If features such as accounts or cloud sharing are added, this policy and Google Play Data safety disclosures will be updated. The current version is published on this page.
