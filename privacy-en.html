About Majuva

Majuva is an Android application for creating and managing shopping lists on your device. Its core shopping list features can be used without an internet connection. The app does not provide user accounts, sign-in, cloud synchronization, or list sharing with other users. Advertising services in ad-supported versions may use an internet connection.

Data stored on your device

Majuva stores list IDs and names, selected list icons and colors, last-used timestamps, product IDs, quantities, units, ordering, and purchased status in Android's app-private local storage. List names are user-entered and may contain personal information. Search text, category and tab selection are not part of the persistent list record. The product catalog, icons, and font are bundled with the app.

Use and sharing of data

Your shopping lists and product records are used locally to display, edit, and preserve your lists. These records are not transmitted to GrapeByte Studios' servers or advertising services. Product images are bundled with the application.

In ad-supported versions, third-party advertising services may collect and process technical data as described below.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Ad-supported versions of Majuva may use Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements.

Depending on device settings and user privacy preferences, Google's advertising services may collect, process, and share the following information:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from the IP address.
  • Advertising ID and other device or account identifiers.
  • App and advertising interactions.
  • Performance, crash, and diagnostic information.

This information may be used for advertising delivery, ad personalization, advertising performance measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention.

The contents of your shopping lists are not used for advertising targeting or transmitted to advertising providers.

User consent and privacy preferences

In ad-supported versions, the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) will be used to manage users' advertising and data processing preferences in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

Where required, users will be provided with options to establish or modify their advertising and data processing preferences, or withdraw previously provided consent.

Depending on user preferences, regional requirements, and advertising service configuration, personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads may be displayed.

Non-personalized advertisements may still require the processing of certain technical data for ad delivery, security, and fraud prevention.

Advertising ID and ad personalization preferences can also be managed through Android device settings.

Android system backups

There is no in-app cloud synchronization. However, Android system backup has not been explicitly disabled. Depending on your device, Android version, manufacturer, and settings, local list records and recovery copies may be backed up or transferred by the operating system.

This is not a transfer to GrapeByte Studios. System backups are managed through Android or the relevant backup provider. GrapeByte Studios cannot remotely delete these backups or control their retention period.

Recovery copies of damaged records

When an unreadable or partially damaged record is detected, Majuva may keep a local copy of the original raw record to prevent accidental data loss. This copy may contain list names, items, quantities, and purchased status.

If reading fails, the app offers a retry option rather than overwriting existing records with a default list. Recovery copies have no automatic expiry, and a newer copy may replace an older one. Deleting an item or list does not automatically erase data from an earlier recovery copy.

Retention and deletion

Ordinary list data may remain until you delete it, clear the application's data, or uninstall Majuva. Lists and items can be deleted within the app.

Android Settings > Apps > Majuva > Storage > Clear data removes the main list and recovery records stored on the device (menu names may vary).

Uninstalling the application removes app-private local data. Android system backups are managed separately and may restore this data later.

The app has no account-deletion process because it does not provide user accounts. GrapeByte Studios cannot remotely erase shopping lists on your device.

The retention and deletion of data processed by Google through advertising services are subject to Google's relevant privacy policies and data management practices.

Security

Records are stored in Android's app-private storage. The application does not implement an additional encryption layer; device locking and Android security protections are important. Absolute security and recovery in every circumstance cannot be guaranteed.

You may request deletion of your correspondence at the same email address, subject to applicable legal retention requirements. No fixed automatic deletion period is promised.
